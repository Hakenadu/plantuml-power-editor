PlantUML Editor

Free online PlantUML editor: write UML diagrams as text in your browser and see them render live – with syntax highlighting, autocompletion and click-to-style editing.

  • Live preview that keeps the last valid diagram while you type
  • Errors highlighted at the affected line
  • Context-aware autocompletion for sequence, class, activity, state and more
  • Click an element to jump to its source or change colors, fonts and lines
  • PlantUML themes, hand-drawn look and your own saved themes
  • Export as PNG or SVG; diagrams are saved locally in your browser
